# Code of conduct

This project is about a children's game, and we'd like working on it to feel
as friendly as the game does. Everyone is welcome: whatever your age,
background, experience, language or the way you write code.

## What we expect

* Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their
  second (or third) language.
* Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming.
* Criticise ideas and code, never people. Explain the *why* when you disagree.
* Accept feedback gracefully, and give credit where it's due.
* Keep it suitable for a project that kids' games are made in.

## What isn't OK

* Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
* Discriminatory jokes or language, and sexual content or advances.
* Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
* Anything else that would make people feel unwelcome or unsafe.

## If something happens

Tell the maintainers: contact [@kinglevel](https://github.com/kinglevel)
privately on GitHub, or use GitHub's *Report content* option on the comment or
issue. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove
comments, close discussions, and block people who don't respect this code of
conduct.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
